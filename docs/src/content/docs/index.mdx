title: Threadifier

**Threadifier** keeps topic-strict group chats tidy. When a digression starts, a moderator forwards the offtopic messages into a linked helper chat. The bot rebuilds them as a reply thread, cleans up both chats, and posts a redirect notice so everyone knows where to continue.

Use the public instance: [@ThreadifierBot](https://t.me/ThreadifierBot). Add it to your main chat and helper chat, then follow [For operators](./operators/) to pair them.

The bot ships translations for **English**, **Spanish**, **French**, and **Russian**. `/language` sets the language for the current chat. Operators may also customize the wording.

## Who this guide is for

| Guide | You are… |
|-------|----------|
| [For members](./members/) | Someone who chats in the main or helper group |
| [For moderators](./moderators/) | Someone who forwards offtopic clusters to relocate them |
| [For operators](./operators/) | An admin who installs the bot, grants rights, and pairs chats |

## How it works (short)

1. A moderator selects offtopic messages in the **main chat** and forwards them to the **helper chat**.
2. The bot posts a thread header in the helper chat, copies the other messages into that thread (each keeps its author’s name), and deletes the temporary forwards.
3. The bot deletes the originals in the main chat and posts a notice with a link to the new thread.
